## A. PRE-TEST (15 questions · 15 min · 답안 회수 후 채점)

> 목적: 수준 편차 파악 → 조 편성 + 교육효과 측정 기준선.
> 사후시험과 **동일 난이도·다른 문항**으로 설계했습니다.

1. How long is the normal estrous cycle in cattle? (a) 14 d (b) **21 d** (c) 28 d (d) 35 d
2. Ovulation occurs approximately how long after the **onset of standing estrus**? (a) 6 h (b) 12 h (c) **24–32 h** (d) 60 h
3. PGF2α will cause luteolysis only when: (a) the cow is pregnant (b) **a functional CL is present (Day 6–17)** (c) any day of the cycle (d) the cow is anestrus
4. GnRH acts by: (a) destroying the CL (b) **causing an LH surge → ovulation / new follicular wave** (c) supplying progesterone (d) inducing estrus signs directly
5. A CIDR device supplies: (a) estrogen (b) prostaglandin (c) **progesterone** (d) FSH
6. In the AM–PM rule, a cow first seen in standing heat at 07:00 should be inseminated: (a) immediately (b) **that afternoon** (c) next morning (d) after 48 h
7. Which IETS stages are normally suitable for transfer? (a) 1–3 (b) **4–7** (c) 8–9 only (d) any stage
8. Which IETS grade should be used for **frozen export** embryos? (a) **Grade 1** (b) Grade 2 (c) Grade 3 (d) Grade 4
9. Correct thawing for a **direct-transfer** frozen embryo straw: (a) 20 °C for 2 min (b) **37 °C for 20–30 sec** (c) room temperature 10 min (d) 45 °C for 1 min
10. An embryo must be transferred into the uterine horn: (a) always the right horn (b) always the left horn (c) **on the same side as the CL** (d) opposite to the CL
11. Recommended BCS range (5-point scale) for an embryo recipient: (a) 2.0–2.5 (b) **2.75–3.25** (c) 3.5–4.0 (d) any
12. Earliest reliable ultrasound pregnancy diagnosis: (a) Day 14 (b) Day 21 (c) **Day 28–32** (d) Day 60
13. Retained placenta is defined as membranes not expelled within: (a) 2 h (b) 6 h (c) **12–24 h** (d) 7 days
14. Heat stress damages the oocyte for approximately: (a) 1–2 days (b) 7 days (c) **40–60 days** (d) no effect
15. Which is **zoonotic** (transmissible to humans)? (a) ketosis (b) displaced abomasum (c) **leptospirosis** (d) sole ulcer

## B. POST-TEST (15 questions · 15 min · 즉시 해설)

1. Standing estrus in tropical/crossbred cattle is often: (a) longer than 24 h (b) **shorter than 8 h** (c) unchanged (d) absent entirely
2. In Ovsynch, the interval between the **second GnRH and fixed-time AI** is: (a) 4–6 h (b) **16–20 h** (c) 36 h (d) 56 h
3. In Ovsynch, PGF2α is given on: (a) Day 0 (b) Day 3 (c) **Day 7** (d) Day 12
4. Best protocol for **anestrus, thin cows and heifers**: (a) double PGF (b) **5-day CO-Synch + CIDR (± eCG)** (c) single PGF (d) no protocol
5. eCG is added at device removal mainly to: (a) cause luteolysis (b) **improve follicle growth / ovulation in anestrus or low-BCS animals** (c) supply progesterone (d) detect heat
6. For a Day-7 embryo, the recipient must be at: (a) Day 0 (b) Day 3 (c) **Day 6–8 after estrus/ovulation** (d) Day 14
7. How many recipients should be synchronized per embryo? (a) 1.0 (b) **1.4–1.6** (c) 3.0 (d) 0.5
8. Sexed embryos produce approximately what proportion of female calves? (a) 100% (b) **~90%** (c) 70% (d) 50%
9. An ease score of 3 (difficult, blood) means: (a) no effect (b) **expect reduced pregnancy; review technique** (c) transfer again immediately (d) the embryo is defective
10. The **single most common** cause of ET failure in the field is: (a) embryo quality (b) **poor recipient selection** (c) gun brand (d) weather
11. Before synchronizing a herd of thin, anestrus cows you should first: (a) increase the hormone dose (b) **correct nutrition / energy balance** (c) use double PGF (d) transfer embryos
12. Hormones should be stored at: (a) −20 °C (b) **2–8 °C, not frozen, away from sunlight** (c) room temperature in the truck (d) 37 °C
13. A cow with foul, watery red-brown uterine discharge at 10 days postpartum most likely has: (a) ketosis (b) **metritis** (c) milk fever (d) ovarian cyst
14. Which practice is **mandatory** to avoid spreading disease between farms? (a) same sleeve if it looks clean (b) **new sleeve and new sanitary sheath for every animal** (c) wash gun once a week (d) visit sick herds first
15. Under heat stress, all handling, AI and ET should be done: (a) at midday (b) **before 09:00** (c) at 15:00 (d) timing does not matter

**채점 기준:** 12/15 이상 = Pass (수료) · 9–11 = Conditional (사후 온라인 보충 후 수료) · 8 이하 = Attendance certificate only.
**효과 지표:** 평균 점수 상승폭 (Post − Pre)을 사후 보고서에 기재 → 국제협력 실적자료로 사용.

## C. 실기 체크리스트 (Practical Skills Checklist · 관찰평가)

관찰자: 강사 또는 보조강사 · 각 항목 ✅/❌ · **핵심항목(★) 실패 시 재교육 필요 표기**

**ST1 · Straw handling & thawing**
- [ ] Reads straw label correctly (donor / sire / stage-grade / sex) ★
- [ ] Identifies straw in the tank neck without warming the goblet
- [ ] Air 3–5 sec, then **37 °C for 20–30 sec** with timer ★
- [ ] Dries and cuts the sealed end square

**ST2 · Gun loading & hygiene**
- [ ] Correct gun assembly, straw seated fully
- [ ] **Uses a new sanitary sheath** ★
- [ ] Keeps the loaded gun protected from sun/wind
- [ ] States the 5–10 minute limit

**ST3 · Ovary / CL evaluation**
- [ ] Identifies CL vs follicle vs cyst on specimen or model ★
- [ ] States the CL side correctly ★
- [ ] States "No CL → no transfer"

**ST4 · Records**
- [ ] Completes all 14 fields of the record line ★
- [ ] Assigns an ease score

**Transfer sequence (verbal or model)**
- [ ] Epidural before manipulation
- [ ] Confirms CL side immediately before insertion ★
- [ ] Deposits in the **cranial 1/3 of the ipsilateral horn** ★

**판정:** ★항목 7개 중 6개 이상 통과 = 실기 Pass.
