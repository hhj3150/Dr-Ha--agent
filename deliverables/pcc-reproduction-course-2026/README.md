## Scope (basics → AI → embryo transfer → smart farm)
1. **Foundations** — female anatomy, the estrous cycle (21-day), hormonal control (GnRH/FSH/LH/PG), follicular waves, the sire side
2. **Estrus & heat detection** — signs, detection methods, AM/PM timing, synchronization
3. **Artificial insemination** — why AI, semen collection→freezing, handling/thawing, recto-vaginal technique, Ovsynch timed-AI, conception-rate troubleshooting
4. **Embryo transfer & IVF** — MOET, superovulation, recovery & grading, OPU–IVF pipeline, sexed semen/embryos, recipient transfer
5. **Smart farming** — sensor heat/health detection, reproduction dashboard, precision-breeding model, KPIs

## Buffalo (carabao) tailoring
Cattle principles throughout, with water-buffalo specifics highlighted (silent/seasonal heat, lower AI conception, OPU–IVF over superovulation, sensors & timed-AI as the practical answer). Ties directly to the PCC herd-improvement mission via Murrah/river-type upgrading.
