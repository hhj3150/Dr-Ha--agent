# PART A — PHYSIOLOGY AND TOOLS (S1–S12, 80분)

## S1. Title
**[EN]** ESTRUS SYNCHRONIZATION & FIXED-TIME AI — Making reproduction a schedule, not a hope
**[KR]** 한 줄 프레임: **"발정을 기다리지 말고, 발정을 만들어라."** ⏱2'

## S2. The problem, in your own farms
**[EN]** Ask yourself:
- How many cows do you inseminate per week — and how many did you **miss**?
- Do you check for heat at night? (60–70% of standing estrus occurs between 18:00 and 06:00)
- In hot months, how short is standing heat? (**often < 8 hours in tropical/crossbred cattle**)
- How many "not pregnant, not cycling" cows are sitting in the barn right now?
**[KR]** 청중 참여로 시작. 손 든 수를 적어두고 S22에서 다시 언급. ⏱6'

## S3. The estrous cycle — 21 days, four things to remember
**[EN]**
| | |
|---|---|
| Cycle length | **21 days (range 18–24)**; heifers slightly shorter |
| Standing estrus | 8–18 h (shorter in heat stress, high yield, crossbreds) |
| Ovulation | **~24–32 h after onset of standing estrus** (≈10–14 h after estrus ends) |
| CL functional (PGF-responsive) | **Day 6 to Day 17** |
> Four hormones run everything: **GnRH → LH/FSH → ovulation**, **Progesterone (P4) = brake**,
> **PGF2α = removes the brake (luteolysis)**, **Estradiol = signs of heat**.
**[KR]** 이 표 하나로 이후 모든 프로토콜이 설명됩니다. 반드시 판서 병행. ⏱10'

## S4. Follicular waves — why timing works
**[EN]**
- Cattle grow follicles in **2–3 waves per cycle**. Each wave has a **dominant follicle**.
- A dominant follicle only ovulates when **P4 is low** (CL gone).
- **GnRH** ovulates or luteinizes the current dominant follicle → **starts a new, synchronized wave**.
- That is the entire logic of Ovsynch: *reset the wave (GnRH) → remove the CL (PGF) → time ovulation (GnRH) → inseminate.*
**[KR]** "GnRH = 리셋 버튼, PGF = 브레이크 해제, 2차 GnRH = 배란 스위치." 3구호. ⏱8'

## S5. Tool 1 — PGF2α (dinoprost / cloprostenol)
**[EN]**
- **Action:** luteolysis → P4 falls → estrus in **2–5 days** (most at 48–96 h).
- **Absolute rule:** **only works if a functional CL is present (Day 6–17).**
  → No CL = no response. This is the #1 reason "the injection didn't work."
- Typical: IM injection. Follow the label dose for the registered product.
- **Safety: never handle if pregnant or asthmatic — absorbed through skin. Wear gloves.**
**[KR]** 안전 문구는 반드시 소리 내어 읽습니다. 현장에서 여성 작업자 사고 사례가 실제로 있습니다. ⏱7'

## S6. Tool 2 — GnRH (gonadorelin / buserelin / lecirelin)
**[EN]**
- **Action:** LH surge → ovulation or luteinization of the dominant follicle → **new follicular wave**.
- Works at **any stage** of the cycle (unlike PGF).
- Uses: start of Ovsynch, final ovulation trigger, treatment of follicular cysts, Day-5 post-AI support (selected cases).
**[KR]** PGF와의 차이를 반복: "PGF는 조건부, GnRH는 무조건부." ⏱5'

## S7. Tool 3 — Progesterone device (CIDR / PRID)
**[EN]**
- Intravaginal P4 device: supplies an **artificial CL**.
- Critical for: **anestrus cows, thin cows, heifers, post-partum cows with no CL** — i.e. **most tropical problem cows**.
- Prevents premature estrus and improves synchrony; on removal, P4 falls → estrus follows.
- Hygiene: clean applicator between animals; discard used devices properly.
**[KR]** 필리핀 조건(저BCS·무발정 다발)에서 **CIDR 기반 프로토콜이 사실상 표준**이라는 점을 명확히. ⏱7'

## S8. Tool 4 — eCG (PMSG) and others
**[EN]**
- **eCG**: FSH/LH-like; used at device removal in **anestrus / low-BCS / suckled** animals to improve
  follicle growth and ovulation rate. Very relevant in tropical low-condition herds.
- **Estradiol esters**: used in some countries for wave synchronization — **availability and legality vary
  by country. Use only if registered in the Philippines.**
- **hCG**: LH-like alternative to GnRH in some protocols.
**[KR]** 에스트라디올은 나라마다 규제가 달라 반드시 "현지 등록 확인" 단서를 붙입니다. ⏱5'

## S9. Cold chain & handling — the invisible failure
**[EN]**
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Storage | Follow label. Most hormones **2–8 °C**, do not freeze, keep out of sunlight |
| Transport | Cool box with ice pack (**not in direct contact**), never in a hot vehicle cabin |
| Field carry | Insulated bag; do not leave syringes on the truck dashboard |
| Opened vial | Record open date; observe the label's in-use shelf life |
| Injection | **IM**, correct needle, **new needle per animal** where possible, clean site |
| Record | Product, batch/lot, dose, date, time, animal ID, operator |
> A perfect protocol with a hormone that sat in a hot car is a **failed protocol**.
**[KR]** 열대지역 최대 실패요인 중 하나. 슬라이드 1장을 통째로 쓸 가치가 있습니다. ⏱6'

## S10. Heat detection — still needed, still worth improving
**[EN]** If you are not doing full FTAI, you must detect heat properly:
- **Primary sign = standing to be mounted.** Everything else is secondary.
- Observe **≥3 times/day, ≥20 minutes each**, at quiet times (early morning, late evening).
- Aids: tail chalk / heat-mount detectors / activity monitors / rumen bolus sensors.
- **AM–PM rule:** heat seen in the morning → inseminate that afternoon; heat seen in the evening →
  inseminate the next morning. (Target: **inseminate ~12 h after onset of standing estrus.**)
**[KR]** FTAI를 가르치더라도 발정발견 기본은 반드시 짚습니다. 여기가 CowTalk 연결 지점(S21). ⏱7'

## S11. Silent heat and anestrus — the tropical reality
**[EN]** Causes to check **before** blaming the hormone:
1. **Negative energy balance / low BCS** — the single biggest cause
2. Heat stress → shortened, weaker estrus expression
3. Mineral deficiency (P, Cu, Se, Zn), parasites, chronic disease
4. Uterine infection (metritis/endometritis) blocking cyclicity
5. Slippery flooring → cows will not mount even when in heat
6. Poor observation (not the cow's fault)
> **Synchronization cannot fix a starving or sick cow. Feed first, then synchronize.**
**[KR]** 이 문장이 오늘 M2에서 가장 중요한 한 줄입니다. 반복 강조. ⏱8'

## S12. Choosing a strategy — the decision tree
**[EN]**
```
Is the animal cycling (CL present, estrus recorded)?
├─ YES → Heifer?  ─ YES → 5-day CO-Synch + CIDR  or  double PGF
│                 └ NO  → Ovsynch / Presynch-Ovsynch (lactating cow)
└─ NO (anestrus / thin / >60 DIM no heat)
        → CIDR-based protocol (+ eCG at removal) — P4 priming is mandatory
Purpose = EMBRYO TRANSFER recipient?  → FTET protocol (S18), select on Day-7 CL
```
**[KR]** 워크숍(M2-B)의 판단 기준이 이 트리입니다. 인쇄본 배포. ⏱9'

# PART B — PROTOCOLS & WORKSHOP (S13–S22, 50분)

## S13. Protocol 1 — Double PGF2α (simplest, cycling animals only)
**[EN]**
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | PGF2α |
| 11–14 | PGF2α (second dose) |
| — | AI on observed estrus, **or** TAI at **72 h and 96 h**, or single TAI ~72–80 h + GnRH |
**Use when:** animals are confirmed cycling, budget is limited, heat detection is possible.
**Do NOT use when:** anestrus, thin cows, unknown cycle status. (No CL → no response.)
**[KR]** 가장 싸지만 무발정 소에는 무용지물. 필리핀에서 실패가 잦은 이유가 여기 있습니다. ⏱5'

## S14. Protocol 2 — Ovsynch (the standard FTAI for lactating cows)
**[EN]**
| Day | Time | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | AM | **GnRH #1** |
| 7 | AM | **PGF2α** |
| 9 | AM (**56 h after PGF**) | **GnRH #2** |
| 10 | **16–20 h after GnRH #2** | **Fixed-Time AI** |
- No heat detection required. Every cow is inseminated on a known date.
- Works best when started in the **Day 5–12 window** of the cycle → hence Presynch.
**[KR]** 참가자들이 가장 원하는 표. 날짜·시간을 판서로 한 번 더 씁니다. ⏱7'

## S15. Protocol 3 — Presynch-Ovsynch (better results in cows)
**[EN]**
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| −26 | PGF2α |
| −12 | PGF2α |
| 0 | GnRH #1 (start Ovsynch, 12–14 days after 2nd presynch PGF) |
| 7 | PGF2α |
| 9 | GnRH #2 (56 h) |
| 10 | **FTAI (16–20 h later)** |
**Why:** starts Ovsynch when more cows have a responsive CL → higher pregnancy per AI.
*(Double-Ovsynch is a further refinement used in high-producing herds.)*
**[KR]** 고능력 젖소 농장용. 필리핀 중소농장에는 CIDR 계열이 더 현실적임을 함께 안내. ⏱5'

## S16. Protocol 4 — ★ 5-day CO-Synch + CIDR (heifers & anestrus — best fit for tropics)
**[EN]**
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | **GnRH + insert CIDR** |
| 5 | **Remove CIDR + PGF2α** *(second PGF 6–12 h later is commonly used in cows)* |
| 8 | **GnRH + Fixed-Time AI** (≈**72 h** after CIDR removal; heifers often ~60 h) |
- Optional: **eCG at CIDR removal** for anestrus / low-BCS / suckled animals.
**Use when:** heifers, anestrus cows, thin cows, unknown cycle status — **the typical Philippine case.**
**[KR]** ★ 이 프로토콜을 오늘의 "기본값(default)"으로 각인시킵니다. 포켓카드 1면에 배치. ⏱8'

## S17. Protocol 5 — 7-day CIDR-Synch
**[EN]**
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | GnRH + CIDR in |
| 7 | CIDR out + PGF2α |
| 9–10 | GnRH + **FTAI at ~56–72 h** after removal |
**Use when:** you want a longer P4 priming for deeply anestrus animals.
**[KR]** 5일법과의 선택 기준: 무발정 정도가 심할수록 7일 프라이밍. ⏱4'

## S18. Protocol 6 — ★★ FTET: synchronizing RECIPIENTS for embryo transfer
**[EN]** The recipient must be at **Day 7 (±1)** after estrus/ovulation on transfer day.
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | **GnRH + CIDR in** |
| 7 | **CIDR out + PGF2α** |
| 9–10 | **GnRH** (ovulation trigger) — record this as **Day 0 of the recipient cycle** |
| **+7 from ovulation** | **Palpate / scan the ovary → CL present?** |
| | **CL YES → TRANSFER (ipsilateral horn)** · **CL NO → REJECT, do not thaw** |
- Expected usable rate: roughly **60–80% of synchronized recipients** have an acceptable Day-7 CL
  (varies with BCS, health and season — verify with your own records).
- **Always synchronize more recipients than embryos.** Plan **~1.4–1.6 recipients per embryo.**
**[KR]** ★★ M1과 M2를 잇는 핵심 슬라이드. "수정란 10개를 보내면 수란우 15두를 준비하라"로 마무리.
이 숫자가 곧 우리 수출 배치 계획의 기준이 됩니다. ⏱9'

## S19. Resynch — never lose a cycle
**[EN]**
- Not pregnant at Day 30–32 check → **re-enter the synchronization program immediately.**
- Common approach: GnRH ~Day 25 → PD at Day 32 → if open, PGF + GnRH + FTAI.
- Every missed cycle = **21 days of lost production and one lost calf-day.**
**[KR]** 재동기화 개념이 없는 농장이 많습니다. "21일을 버리지 마라." ⏱4'

## S20. Common protocol failures — audit list
**[EN]**
| Failure | Root cause |
|---|---|
| "The PGF didn't work" | No functional CL (animal was anestrus or Day 0–5) |
| Poor synchrony after CIDR | Device lost / expelled — **check retention at removal day** |
| Low FTAI conception | Wrong interval between last GnRH and AI (must be **16–20 h**) |
| Whole group fails | Hormone heat-damaged in transport (S9) |
| Good sync, no pregnancy | Semen handling / AI technique / heat stress / uterine infection |
| Works in cool season only | Heat stress — move all handling before 09:00 |
> **Audit in this order: 1) the cow, 2) the hormone's cold chain, 3) the calendar, 4) the technique.**
**[KR]** 이 순서를 외우게 하면 현장 문제해결 능력이 확실히 올라갑니다. ⏱6'

## S21. Bridge — where sensors change the game *(pilot)*
**[EN]**
- Rumen bolus sensors (temperature, activity, rumination, drinking) can flag **estrus, calving onset and
  early illness** without visual observation — including at night.
- In synchronization programs, sensor data helps verify **who actually responded**.
- *Expected benefit; performance in Philippine conditions requires pilot validation.*
**[KR]** CowTalk/smaXtec 연결. 반드시 *expected / pilot* 표기. 가격·공급구조 언급 금지. ⏱3'

## S22. Wrap-up — three rules to take home
**[EN]**
1. **No CL, no PGF.** Know the cycle stage before you inject.
2. **Feed first, then synchronize.** Thin cows do not respond to any protocol.
3. **Write it down.** A protocol without a calendar and a record sheet is a wish, not a program.
**[KR]** S2에서 적어둔 손 든 수를 다시 언급하며 마무리. ⏱2'
