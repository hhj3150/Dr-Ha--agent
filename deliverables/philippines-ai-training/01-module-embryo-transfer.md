**[EN]** EMBRYO TRANSFER FOR TROPICAL DAIRY — From AI Technician to ET Technician
Dr. Hyunje Ha, DVM · Genetics Co., Ltd. / Korea Animal Hospital

## S2. What ET gives you that AI cannot
**[EN]**
| | Artificial Insemination | Embryo Transfer |
|---|---|---|
| Genetics carried | Sire only (50%) | Sire **and** elite dam (100%) |
| Offspring / elite female / year | 1 | 10–30+ (MOET/OPU-IVP) |
| Genetic gain speed | Baseline | **2–4× faster herd upgrade** |
| Can you upgrade a low-merit herd? | Slowly, over generations | **In one generation** |
| Sexed female calves | Sexed semen (~90% F) | Sexed embryo (~90% F) **+ elite dam** |
**[KR]** 핵심 문장 1개만 외우게 함: **"AI improves the sire side. ET replaces the whole animal."** ⏱6'

## S3. Why this matters in the Philippines
**[EN]**
- Local dairy herds are limited by **female genetic base**, not by semen access.
- Importing live heifers = expensive, high transport loss, poor tropical adaptation on arrival.
- Importing **frozen embryos** = low cost per genetic unit, no live-animal transport stress,
  calf is **born and raised in Philippine conditions from day 1** → better adaptation.
- Recipient can be **any healthy local cow/heifer** — including low-merit animals you already own.
**[KR]** 여기서 사업 포인트: "여러분 농장의 능력 낮은 소가 **최고 유전자 송아지의 어미**가 됩니다."
※ 수출 품목은 **한국 홀스타인 성감별(암) 젖소 수정란**으로만 설명. 한우 유전자원은 언급 금지. ⏱6'

## S4. The economics — one slide, one number
**[EN]** Cost per pregnancy = (embryo cost + synchronization cost + technician fee) ÷ pregnancy rate
Example (illustrative only, confirm local prices):
- Inputs 100 units, pregnancy rate 50% → **200 units per pregnancy**
- Same inputs, pregnancy rate 60% → **167 units per pregnancy** (−17%)
> **The cheapest way to lower cost is to raise pregnancy rate — not to buy cheaper embryos.**
**[KR]** 숫자를 화이트보드에 직접 쓰면서. 오늘 강의 전체의 논리 = 수태율. ⏱5'

## S5. Where embryos come from — two routes
**[EN]**
| | **In-vivo (MOET / flushing)** | **In-vitro (OPU–IVP)** |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Superovulated donor, uterine flush D7 | Ovum pick-up → lab fertilization → culture D7 |
| Embryos per session | ~5–8 transferable (variable) | ~2–6 per OPU, but repeatable every 1–2 weeks |
| Freezing tolerance | Good (slow freeze, direct transfer) | Lower — often **vitrified** |
| Sexing | Sexed semen or biopsy | **Sexed semen at IVF — standard** |
| Typical use | Proven elite donors | High-throughput, sexed female production |
**[KR]** 우리(제네틱스)는 두 경로 모두 보유. 수출용은 성감별 IVP 중심. 실험실 상세는 다루지 않음. ⏱7'

## S6. Reading the straw — you MUST be able to do this
**[EN]** Straw label typically shows: Donor ID · Sire ID · **Stage** · **Grade** · Sex · Freeze date · Method (DT / vitrified) · Lab code
> **If you cannot read the straw, do not transfer it.**
**[KR]** 실물 스트로(공 스트로) 돌려보게 함. 라벨 판독 실습 30초. ⏱4'

**[KR]** 실무 결론: **Stage 4–7이 이식 대상.** 6–7이 동결내성·수태율 최상. ⏱6'

## S8. IETS quality grade
**[EN]**
| Grade | Description | Expected relative pregnancy |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Excellent** | Symmetrical, uniform cells, ≥85% intact mass, clear zona | 100% (reference) |
| **2 Good** | Minor irregularities, ≥50% intact | ~85–90% of Grade 1 |
| **3 Fair** | Major irregularities, ≥25% intact | ~60–70% of Grade 1 |
| **4 Poor / Degenerate** | Do not transfer, do not freeze | — |
**[KR]** 현장 규칙: **동결·수출용은 Grade 1 only.** Grade 2는 신선 이식 한정. 우리가 Grade 1만 보내는 이유를 강조. ⏱5'

## S9. Sexed female embryos — what "90%" really means
**[EN]**
- Sexed semen at IVF gives approximately **90% female** calves (industry standard range).
- 90% ≠ 100%. In a batch of 10 pregnancies, **expect ~1 male**. This is normal, not a defect.
- Value in dairy: every female calf is a future milking cow + a future recipient.
**[KR]** 클레임 예방 슬라이드입니다. 반드시 사전에 말해두어야 나중에 분쟁이 없습니다. ⏱4'

## S10. ★ THE RECIPIENT IS THE PRODUCT — 7 selection criteria
**[EN]** Screen every candidate against all 7. **One failure = reject.**
| # | Criterion | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cycling status | Observed / recorded estrus at least once; not anestrus |
| 2 | Body condition score | **2.75–3.25 (5-point scale)** — not thin, not fat |
| 3 | Days postpartum | **≥ 50–60 days**, uterus fully involuted |
| 4 | Reproductive tract | Normal palpation/US: no pyometra, no adhesion, no persistent CL abnormality |
| 5 | Health | No lameness, no fever, no chronic mastitis, no active disease |
| 6 | Age / parity | Heifers ≥ 14–15 months & target breeding weight; cows parity 1–5 |
| 7 | **CL on Day 7** | **Palpable / US-confirmed functional CL** — this decides GO / NO-GO |
> A Grade-1 embryo in a bad recipient = wasted embryo. **The recipient is 50%+ of the result.**
**[KR]** 이 슬라이드가 M1의 심장입니다. 시간을 가장 많이 쓰세요.
필리핀 현장 강조: BCS 미달·산후 60일 미만·무발정 소를 수란우로 쓰는 것이 실패의 1번 원인. ⏱12'

## S11. Day-7 CL check — GO / NO-GO
**[EN]**
- Recipient must be at **Day 6–8 after estrus** when a Day-7 embryo is transferred (±1 day tolerance).
- Confirm **CL ≥ ~18–20 mm** (or clear functional CL by ultrasound) on the ovary.
- **Transfer into the horn IPSILATERAL to the CL** — same side. Always.
- No CL, weak CL, or cystic ovary → **do not transfer. Return the embryo to the tank.**
**[KR]** "No CL, no transfer." 구호처럼 3번 복창시킵니다. ⏱7'

## S12. Tropical reality — heat stress and why ET wins
**[EN]**
- Heat stress damages the **oocyte for 40–60 days** before ovulation → AI conception falls in hot season.
- In ET, the oocyte was matured in a **cool, controlled environment (Korea / laboratory)**.
  The recipient only has to carry a Day-7 embryo — past the most heat-sensitive window.
- Practical: this is why **ET often outperforms AI in the hot season** in tropical herds.
- Still required in the recipient: shade, fans, water, transfer in **early morning (before 09:00)**.
**[KR]** 필리핀에서 ET를 파는 가장 강력한 과학적 논리입니다. *현지 성적은 pilot target으로 표기.* ⏱7'

# PART B — HOW: HANDS AND RESULTS (S13–S24, 70분)

## S13. Equipment checklist
**[EN]** LN2 tank & goblet map · thermometer · **37°C water bath** · timer · straw cutter/scissors ·
**ET gun (French 0.25 mL) + sanitary sheath + outer chemise** · lidocaine 2% + syringe/needle for epidural ·
disposable sleeves · paper towel · 70% alcohol · tail rope · **records sheet** · ultrasound (if available)
> **One sanitary sheath per cow. No exception.**
**[KR]** 위생 시스(sheath) 재사용이 현지에서 흔한 문제. 여기서 단호하게 못 박습니다. ⏱4'

## S14. Thawing — Direct Transfer (DT, ethylene glycol) frozen embryos
**[EN]** Standard sequence:
1. Locate straw in tank, identify label **while still in the neck of the tank**.
2. Remove straw → hold in **air 3–5 seconds**.
3. Plunge into **37°C water bath for 20–30 seconds**.
4. Dry straw completely, wipe with clean towel/alcohol, cut the sealed end **square**.
5. Load into pre-warmed ET gun, cover with sanitary sheath.
6. **Transfer within 5–10 minutes.** Protect from direct sunlight and wind.
> DT embryos go **straight into the uterus** — no washing, no dish work.
**[KR]** 실습 스테이션 ST1과 연결. 타이머를 실제로 눌러 보여줍니다. ⏱8'

## S15. Vitrified embryos — different rules
**[EN]**
- Vitrified (ultra-rapid frozen, common for **IVP** embryos) requires a **stepwise warming / dilution**
  protocol supplied by the producing laboratory.
- **Never** apply the DT protocol to a vitrified straw.
- Rule: **follow the laboratory's written protocol that ships with the batch.**
**[KR]** 우리 배치 출하 시 **영문 해동 프로토콜 카드 동봉**을 표준화해야 함 → 랩 운영 액션아이템. ⏱4'

## S16. Transfer technique — step by step
**[EN]**
1. Restrain in chute. Tail held aside (assistant or tail rope).
2. **Epidural: 2–3 mL of 2% lidocaine** (sacrococcygeal). Wait for tail relaxation.
3. Clean the vulva and perineum thoroughly; dry.
4. Rectal palpation: **confirm the CL side again**.
5. Insert gun; pass the cervix **gently** — the cervix is manipulated onto the gun, not the gun forced through.
6. Advance into the **ipsilateral uterine horn**; break the sanitary sheath at the internal os.
7. Deposit in the **cranial (front) one-third of the horn**. Push the plunger slowly and fully.
8. Withdraw straight and smoothly. Check the straw is empty.
9. **Record immediately**: cow ID, straw ID, CL side, ease score (1 easy – 3 difficult), operator, time.
**[KR]** 강조 3가지: ①경관 통과 시 힘 쓰지 말 것 ②황체측 자궁각 ③선단 1/3. ⏱12'

## S17. Ease score — a number that predicts your pregnancy rate
**[EN]**
| Score | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Passed easily, no blood | Normal |
| 2 | Some resistance, no blood | Review technique / lubrication |
| 3 | **Difficult, blood on sheath, repeated attempts** | Expect reduced pregnancy; retrain, review restraint |
> Track your personal % of Score 3. If it is above ~10%, your technique is the problem, not the embryos.
**[KR]** 자기 기록으로 자기 술기를 개선하게 만드는 슬라이드. 수정사들이 가장 좋아합니다. ⏱5'

## S18. ★ Top 8 reasons ET fails — in field order
**[EN]**
| # | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Poor recipient** (BCS, anestrus, dirty uterus) | Apply the 7 criteria (S10) without exception |
| 2 | **No / weak CL on Day 7** | Confirm before thawing. No CL, no transfer |
| 3 | Wrong horn (contralateral) | Re-palpate right before insertion |
| 4 | Thaw errors (wrong temp/time, delay >10 min) | Water bath + timer, every time |
| 5 | Cervical trauma / difficult passage | Epidural, lubrication, patience, ease-score tracking |
| 6 | Contamination (sheath reuse, dirty perineum) | One sheath per cow, clean & dry |
| 7 | **Heat stress at/after transfer** | Transfer before 09:00; shade, fans, water |
| 8 | Nutrition / negative energy balance after transfer | Do not change ration abruptly; keep BCS stable to Day 45 |
**[KR]** 여기서 참가자에게 "본인 농장에서 몇 번이 문제인가?" 손 들게 합니다. ⏱9'

## S19. After transfer — the first 45 days decide
**[EN]**
- **Do not** move, regroup, transport, vaccinate, or dehorn recipients in the first 7–10 days if avoidable.
- Keep feeding and housing **unchanged**. Stable BCS. Clean water, always.
- Heat abatement is a pregnancy tool, not a comfort item.
- Watch for **return to estrus at Day 18–24** — record it; a return = a loss, and it tells you where the problem is.
**[KR]** "이식은 5분, 임신은 45일" — 문장으로 남깁니다. ⏱5'

## S20. Pregnancy diagnosis
**[EN]**
| Method | Earliest | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ultrasound (transrectal) | **Day 28–32** | Confirm heartbeat; recheck **Day 55–60** for embryonic loss |
| Rectal palpation | Day 40–45 | Operator-dependent; risk of iatrogenic loss if rough |
| Blood PAG / chemical test | ~Day 28+ | Where lab access exists |
> Always do the **second check at Day 55–60**. Early embryonic loss is real, especially in hot season.
**[KR]** 임신진단 2회 확인이 데이터 신뢰도를 만듭니다. 우리 사후관리 서비스 연결 포인트. ⏱5'

## S23. Buffalo Note — carabao ET
**[EN]**
- Buffalo (*Bubalus bubalis*) have **fewer antral follicles**, lower superovulation response, and a
  **higher rate of silent estrus** than cattle → recipient synchronization is even more critical.
- Cervix is more tortuous → passage is harder; **epidural and patience are mandatory**.
- Seasonality: buffalo show stronger seasonal reproductive patterns; plan programs accordingly.
- Buffalo embryos must come from buffalo donors — **cattle embryos are not transferred into buffalo.**
**[KR]** 필리핀 수정사 상당수가 carabao(PCC)를 다룹니다. 이 슬라이드 하나로 신뢰도가 크게 올라갑니다.
※ 우리 수출 품목은 젖소(홀스타인) 수정란이며, 물소는 현장 이해를 돕는 기술 설명입니다. ⏱5'

## 부록 B · 자주 나오는 질문 (강사 준비 답변)

**Q1. "동결수정란과 신선수정란 중 뭐가 낫나?"**
→ 신선이 수태율은 조금 높지만, 국가 간 수출·일정 유연성·재고 관리는 동결만 가능. 필리핀 조건에서는 **동결 DT가 현실적 선택**.

**Q2. "우리 소는 마른데 이식해도 되나?"**
→ 안 됩니다. BCS 2.75 미만은 임신 유지 실패율이 급격히 올라갑니다. **먼저 사양을, 다음에 수정란을.**

**Q3. "성감별인데 수컷이 나왔다. 왜인가?"**
→ 성감별 정액 기준 약 90% 암컷. 10마리 중 1마리 수컷은 정상 범위입니다(S9).

**Q4. "이식 후 언제 다시 수정할 수 있나?"**
→ Day 18–24 재발정 확인 → 재발정 시 다음 주기에 재이식 또는 AI. 임신진단(Day 28–32) 전 무리한 촉진은 금물.

**Q5. "수정란이 비싸다."**
→ 계산은 개당 가격이 아니라 **임신 1두당 비용**으로 합니다(S4). 수태율 10%p 상승 = 비용 약 17% 절감.
