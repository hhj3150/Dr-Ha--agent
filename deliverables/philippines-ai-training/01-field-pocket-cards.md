## CARD 2 — THAWING & TRANSFER (Direct Transfer straws)

**THAW**
1. Read the label **in the tank neck**
2. Air **3–5 sec**
3. **37 °C water bath — 20–30 sec**
4. Dry · wipe · cut sealed end square
5. Load gun + **sanitary sheath (one per cow)**
6. **Transfer within 5–10 min** · no sunlight, no wind

⚠️ **VITRIFIED straws: DO NOT use this protocol.** Follow the laboratory card in the batch.

**TRANSFER**
1. Restrain · tail aside
2. **Epidural 2–3 mL lidocaine 2%**
3. Clean & dry vulva
4. Palpate — **confirm CL side**
5. Pass cervix **gently** (move cervix onto the gun)
6. Enter **ipsilateral horn**, break sheath at internal os
7. Deposit in **cranial 1/3** of the horn · slow, full push
8. Withdraw straight · check straw is empty
9. **RECORD NOW**

**EASE SCORE** 1 = easy · 2 = resistance · 3 = **difficult / blood** → expect lower pregnancy

## CARD 3 — ESTRUS CYCLE ESSENTIALS

| Cycle length | **21 d** (18–24) |
|---|---|
| Standing estrus | 8–18 h (**shorter in heat stress**) |
| Ovulation | **24–32 h after onset of standing estrus** |
| **PGF2α works only** | **Day 6 – Day 17 (functional CL)** |
| GnRH works | **Any day** |
| AI timing rule | **~12 h after onset of standing heat** (AM → PM · PM → next AM) |

**60–70% of standing heats happen between 18:00 and 06:00.**
Observe **3× daily, 20 min each**, at quiet times.

⛔ **NO CL → PGF WILL NOT WORK.**

## CARD 5 — WHEN NOT TO INSEMINATE OR TRANSFER

### ⛔ CALL THE VETERINARIAN — do nothing today
- Temperature **≥ 39.5 °C** or obvious systemic illness
- Foul-smelling uterine discharge within 21 days postpartum
- Down cow · severe lameness (score 4–5)
- Acute mastitis with systemic signs
- Abortion / stillbirth / abnormal membranes
- Suspected ovarian cyst (>25 mm, no CL)

### ⏸ POSTPONE
- BCS < 2.75 · Less than 50 days postpartum
- On antibiotics / in withdrawal period
- No estrus record (anestrus) → put on a **CIDR-based protocol** first

### ✅ PROCEED
- None of the above **AND** (for ET) **Day-7 CL confirmed**
