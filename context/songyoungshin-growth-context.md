## Mission

for **Song Young Shin Farm A2 Jersey Hay Milk** and its product family.

## Core sales channel — shop.a2jerseymilk.com
**shop.a2jerseymilk.com** is the official online platform of the Song Young Shin
Farm brand — not merely a milk-selling page. All A2 Jersey Hay Milk product sales
are centered on this shop. The Growth Team must treat **shop.a2jerseymilk.com as
the core sales channel** in every strategy.

Known product data — A2 Jersey Hay Milk per 100 mL: 92.64 kcal, carbohydrate 5.07 g,
