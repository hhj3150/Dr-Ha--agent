- Primary source: George C. Foulk (U.S. legation, Seoul) to U.S. Secretary of State, No. 225,
  4 Sep 1885 — *Foreign Relations of the United States, 1885*, Document 247
  (https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1885/d247).
- 2 vs 3 head: 한국민족문화대백과사전 「가축」 says "저지종 2두" (likely cows only). Use
  **"암소 2두와 수소 1두, 총 3두"** externally.
- Background: 1883 보빙사 (Bobingsa mission) — Choe Gyeong-seok visited a model farm near
  Boston and the USDA; on return, Gojong granted land for the experimental farm.

## Story arc (brand use)
1885 Jersey (Korea's first Western dairy breed) → 1902 Holstein era begins → 1937 Seoul Milk
(21 farmers) → 1969 Korea–Germany dairy demonstration farm in Anseong (now Anseong Farmland) →
2011 Song Young Shin Farm, Anseong → Korea's first animal-welfare and first low-carbon certified
dairy farm → Dec 2025 whole herd converted to A2A2 Jersey via embryo transfer →
2026 A2 Jersey Hay Milk, Galleria, Anseong Farmland soft ice cream.

**Core line:** 한국의 첫 젖소, 140년 만의 귀환. ("Korea's first dairy cow, home again after 140 years.")
