Your job is not to treat. Your job is to **see it, record it, and refer it — before you waste a straw.**

## S2. The map — where diseases attack fertility
**[EN]**
```
TRANSITION (−3 wk → +3 wk)      REPRODUCTIVE          WHOLE-COW
milk fever ─┐                   retained placenta      mastitis
ketosis ────┼─→ immune          metritis / endometritis   lameness
```

## PART 1 — TRANSITION PERIOD (이행기 4대 질병)

## S3. Milk fever (hypocalcemia) — 유열
**[EN]**
- **When:** within 0–72 h of calving. Higher risk: parity ≥3, high yield, over-conditioned cows.
- **Signs:** unsteady, cold ears, dry muzzle, **S-shaped neck / sternal recumbency**, weak rumen, no manure.
- **Subclinical form is the bigger problem** — no down cow, but weak uterus and teat sphincter.
- **Fertility impact:** retained placenta ↑, metritis ↑, displaced abomasum ↑, later conception.
- **Prevention:** dry-cow ration low in calcium/potassium (**DCAD** control), Mg adequate, avoid over-fat cows,
  oral Ca supplement at calving in high-risk cows (veterinary guidance).
**[KR]** 수정사에게 중요한 포인트: "이 소는 산후 회복이 늦어 60일 이내 수정 대상이 아니다"를 판단하게 함. ⏱5'

## S4. Ketosis & negative energy balance — 케톤증
**[EN]**
- **When:** peak risk 2–6 weeks after calving.
- **Signs:** drop in feed intake and milk, rapid BCS loss, sweet/acetone breath, dull, firm dry manure.
- **Fertility impact:** the most direct cause of **anestrus, weak estrus, poor oocyte quality, embryo loss.**
- **Field check:** BCS at calving vs 30 DIM. **Loss of >0.5 BCS point = red flag.**
- **Prevention:** avoid fat dry cows (BCS 3.0–3.5 at dry-off), smooth ration transition, adequate bunk space and water.
**[KR]** M2의 "Feed first, then synchronize"와 직접 연결. ⏱5'

## S5. Retained placenta (RP) & metritis — 후산정체·자궁염
**[EN]**
- **RP definition:** fetal membranes not expelled within **12–24 h** after calving.
- **Risk factors:** milk fever, twins, dystocia, abortion, short/long dry period, Se/vitamin E deficiency, heat stress.
- **Metritis:** enlarged uterus + **fetid, watery, red-brown discharge**, within 21 DIM, ± fever, ± drop in milk.
- **Endometritis (subclinical/clinical):** after 21 DIM, purulent/mucopurulent discharge, cow looks normal but **will not conceive**.
- **Fertility impact:** longer days open, lower conception per service, higher embryo loss.
- **Do NOT** manually pull the placenta. Refer to the veterinarian.
**[KR]** "손으로 후산 잡아당기지 마라"는 현지에서 매우 흔한 잘못. 강하게 못 박습니다. ⏱6'

## S6. Displaced abomasum (DA) — 제4위 전위
**[EN]** Signs: sudden milk drop 2–4 weeks post-calving, reduced feed intake, "ping" on left flank auscultation,
often follows ketosis/milk fever/metritis. **Surgical correction — refer immediately.**
**[KR]** 필리핀 소규모 농가에선 상대적으로 덜하나, 고능력 농장 진입 시 급증. 간단히. ⏱2'

## PART 2 — REPRODUCTIVE DISORDERS (번식장애)

## S7. Ovarian cysts — 난소낭종
**[EN]**
| | **Follicular cyst** | **Luteal cyst** |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | >20–25 mm, thin wall, no CL tissue | >20–25 mm, thick wall, luteal tissue |
| P4 | Low | High |
| Signs | Nymphomania or anestrus, irregular cycles | Anestrus |
| Typical treatment (veterinary) | **GnRH** (± CIDR) then PGF | **PGF2α** responds |
**Field rule:** a cystic ovary is **not** a transfer or FTAI candidate until resolved and re-cycled.
**[KR]** M1의 수란우 선발 기준 #4와 연결. ⏱5'

## S8. Anestrus & silent heat — 무발정·미약발정
**[EN]** Checklist before calling it "a hormone problem":
BCS <2.75? · <50 DIM? · uterine infection? · lameness? · mineral deficiency (P, Cu, Se, Zn)? ·
parasites? · heat stress? · slippery floor? · **poor observation?**
> Fix the cause first. Then use a **P4-based protocol (CIDR ± eCG)** — see Module 2, S16.
**[KR]** M2와 의도적으로 중복시켜 각인시킵니다. ⏱4'

## S9. Repeat breeder — 반복수정우
**[EN]** Definition: cycling normally, **≥3 services**, no pregnancy, no gross abnormality.
Investigate in this order:
1. **Semen handling & AI timing** (thaw temperature, straw exposure, deposition site, 12-h rule)
2. **Subclinical endometritis** (cytology / discharge check by vet)
3. **Heat stress / nutrition / mycotoxin**
4. **Infectious causes** (BVD, IBR, lepto, neospora)
5. Anatomic (cervix, adhesion)
> **In repeat breeders, embryo transfer is often the practical solution** — bypasses fertilization failure
> and early oocyte-related loss. *(Case selection by veterinarian.)*
**[KR]** ET 수요를 자연스럽게 만드는 슬라이드. 판매 언어 없이 기술적으로 제시. ⏱5'

## PART 3 — WHOLE-COW DISEASES

## S10. Mastitis — 유방염
**[EN]**
- **Clinical:** abnormal milk (clots/watery), swollen/hot quarter, ± systemic fever.
- **Subclinical:** normal-looking milk, **high SCC** — the hidden majority. Screen with **CMT**.
- **Contagious pathogens** (*Staph. aureus*, *Strep. agalactiae*, *Mycoplasma*) spread cow-to-cow **at milking**.
  **Environmental** (*E. coli*, *Strep. uberis*, *Klebsiella*) come from bedding and wet, dirty housing.
- **Fertility impact:** fever and inflammatory mediators around AI/ET can cause **ovulation failure and embryo loss**.
  A cow with clinical mastitis is **not** a transfer candidate that day.
- **Control basics:** pre/post-dipping, single-use towels, correct milking machine function, **dry-cow therapy**,
  **milk infected cows last**, clean and dry bedding.
**[KR]** D2O 베딩(환경성 유방염 저감) 연결 여지가 있으나, 이번 강의에서는 **원칙만** 다루고 제품 언급은 하지 않습니다.
(필요 시 별도 세션에서 제안 — d2o-peatmoss-commercial 연계.) ⏱6'

## S11. Lameness — 발굽질병
**[EN]**
- Common in wet tropical housing: **digital dermatitis (hairy heel wart), foot rot, sole ulcer, white line disease**.
- **Fertility impact:** lame cows **do not stand to be mounted** → estrus missed; they eat less → NEB → anestrus.
- **Field action:** locomotion score monthly; keep walkways dry; footbath; routine hoof trimming.
> A limping cow is a reproductive problem before it is a foot problem.
**[KR]** 발정발견 실패 원인 중 저평가된 항목. 수정사에게 매우 실용적입니다. ⏱4'

## S12. ★ Heat stress — the Philippine core issue
**[EN]**
- **THI ≥ 72** = production loss begins; **THI ≥ 78** = severe reproductive impact.
- Effects: feed intake ↓, milk ↓, **estrus expression shorter and weaker**, **oocyte damaged for 40–60 days**,
  **early embryonic loss ↑**, calf birth weight ↓.
- **Abatement (in order of return on investment):**
  1. **Water** — enough clean water, multiple points, shaded
  2. **Shade** — over feed bunk and resting area
  3. **Air movement** — fans over feeding and holding area
  4. **Sprinkler + fan combination** at the feed line (wet-then-dry cycle)
  5. **Timing** — do all handling, AI, and ET **before 09:00**
- **This is why embryo transfer can outperform AI in the hot season** (see Module 1, S12).
**[KR]** 필리핀 강의에서 가장 실용적인 슬라이드. 시간 배분 넉넉히. ⏱7'

## S13. Infectious diseases that destroy pregnancies
**[EN]**
| Disease | Reproductive effect | Note for technicians |
|---|---|---|
| **BVD** | Embryonic death, abortion, **persistently infected (PI) calves** | PI animals shed constantly — test & cull |
| **IBR (BoHV-1)** | Abortion, infertility, respiratory | Vaccination programs (veterinary) |
| **Leptospirosis** | Abortion, stillbirth, weak calves | **Zoonotic** — rodents, contaminated water; wear gloves |
| **Brucellosis** | Abortion (late), retained placenta | **Zoonotic, notifiable** — never handle aborted material bare-handed |
| **Neosporosis** | Repeat abortion, embryo loss | Dogs in the barn are the risk; keep dogs away from placenta/fetuses |
| **Trichomoniasis / Campylobacter** | Early embryonic death, pyometra | Bull-transmitted — **AI reduces the risk** |
> **Abortion = report it.** Handle aborted fetuses and membranes with gloves; isolate the cow; call the veterinarian.
**[KR]** 인수공통(lepto, brucella)은 강하게 경고. 필리핀 현장 안전과 직결됩니다. ⏱6'

## S14. Tropical / vector-borne diseases (Philippine context)
**[EN]**
- **Surra (*Trypanosoma evansi*)** — fly-transmitted; fever, weight loss, anemia, **abortion and infertility**;
  significant in Philippine cattle and **carabao**.
- **Tick-borne:** *Babesia*, *Anaplasma*, *Theileria* — anemia, fever, abortion, sudden drops in production.
- **Haemorrhagic septicaemia (*Pasteurella multocida*)** — acute, high mortality in buffalo and cattle; vaccination program.
- **FMD** — status is a national trade issue; follow BAI reporting rules absolutely.
- **Internal/external parasites** — chronic drain on BCS → anestrus.
> **Control the vector, control the fertility.** Fly and tick control is a reproduction program.
**[KR]** 현지 특이 질병을 강사가 알고 있다는 것 자체가 신뢰를 만듭니다. 반드시 포함. ⏱5'

## S15. Calf health — your future recipients and milking herd
**[EN]**
- **Colostrum rule: 3–4 L of good-quality colostrum within the first 2 hours**, clean bucket or tube.
- Main killers: **diarrhea (0–3 wk)** and **pneumonia (3 wk–4 mo)**.
- Clean, dry, well-ventilated, individual housing in early weeks; navel dipping.
> Every ET calf you produce is a **genetic investment**. Losing it at 3 weeks loses the whole program's value.
**[KR]** 수정란 사업 관점에서 반드시 넣어야 하는 슬라이드. 송아지를 잃으면 우리 실적도 사라집니다. ⏱4'

## S16. ★ YOU are a biosecurity risk — the technician's own rules
**[EN]** You visit many farms in one day. You can carry disease between them.
| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | **New sleeve for every cow.** Never reuse. |
| 2 | **New sanitary sheath for every ET.** Never reuse. |
| 3 | Clean and disinfect boots and coveralls **between farms** — carry a brush, water and disinfectant |
| 4 | Clean the AI/ET gun between animals; store in a clean, closed case |
| 5 | Never carry manure, placenta or aborted material off a farm |
| 6 | Visit **healthy/young herds first, problem herds last** in your daily route |
| 7 | Wear gloves when handling any discharge — **lepto and brucella are zoonotic** |
| 8 | Record every farm visit — if disease breaks out, your records protect you |
**[KR]** ★ 이 슬라이드는 참가자 자존감과 직결됩니다. "여러분은 방역의 최전선"이라는 프레임으로 전달. ⏱6'

## 부록 · 현장 판단 카드 (수정사용 REFER 기준)

**즉시 수의사 호출 (Do not inseminate / transfer today):**
- 체온 39.5 °C 이상 또는 명백한 전신증상
- 산후 21일 이내 악취성 자궁분비물
- 기립불능·심한 파행(보행점수 4–5)
- 급성 유방염(전신증상 동반)
- 유산·사산·태아막 배출 이상
- 낭종성 난소 의심 (>25 mm, 황체 없음)

**연기 (Postpone, recheck later):**
- BCS < 2.75
- 산후 50일 미만
- 최근 항생제 투여 중 / 휴약기간 중
- 발정 기록 없음(무발정) — 먼저 동기화 프로그램으로 전환

**진행 가능 (Proceed):**
